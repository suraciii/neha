# Group

## Why

The user needs research, a draft, and a review. In a Group they address the researcher, then the writer, then the reviewer, and each handoff is a Message that everyone in the Group sees.

Some work needs several Bots. Coordination that happens out of sight is coordination the user cannot trust, so a Group is one record in which the whole chain stays visible.

## What

A Group is a conversation among any number of Users and Bots, created by a User; Users and Bots are members alike.

Everything members say to each other in a Group is in the Group, and a handoff is a Message.

A User addresses a Bot with `@`, and everyone with `@all`; a Message that addresses nobody lets the Bots decide who answers.

An approval appears in the Group, and the User the Bot acts for gives it.

## Why this way

One record for everything said keeps the chain of handoffs in one window, and a Bot's work for the Group is visible to every member.

Two ways to be pulled in — addressed, or judging a Message as one's own — cover a direct order and a team deciding, in the same Group.

Approvals stay in the Group because that is where the work was given, and the User who asked is the one who answers.

## Principles

- **Members alike.** A User and a Bot join a Group the same way and read the same record.
- **Everything in the open.** Bots say everything to each other in the Group; a handoff is a Message everyone sees.
- **Addressed or self-judged.** A Bot acts when addressed or when it judges a Message is its to answer.
- **Approval where the work was given.** An approval appears in the Group, and the User the Bot acts for gives it.

## What this enables

- Visible handoffs between Bots.
- A team of Bots for one outcome, with every step in one window.
- Tracking work on top of this shape: a Task is a Message with a state, so a board needs no separate record.
