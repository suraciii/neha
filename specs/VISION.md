# Vision

## Why

Neha exists to be the one place where a user meets their agents and their computers.
It serves anyone who has work to hand off, and it leaves room for anyone who wants to rebuild it.

## What

A Bot is a teammate.

You give a teammate work the way you give work to a colleague, and it returns with a result.

A teammate has its own computer. It signs into your tools and uses them as you do.

A teammate remembers how you work; what it learns in one conversation it carries to the next.

A teammate is always on. It keeps working while you are away, and returns when the work is done or when it needs information, judgment, or authorization.

Teammates work together. They take work in parallel, hand it off, and share what they learn.

## Principles

- **Several users, one product.** Neha serves one user or many with the same model; using it alone simplifies what is shown, and the model is the same either way.
- **You own it.** The complete product runs on infrastructure the user running it controls, and every provider is that user's choice.
- **It lives where you choose.** A teammate's actions run on a Hosted Computer or on a Local Computer a User controls; on the Local one, its state is the machine's own. The machine's owner gives permission.
- **It can be rebuilt.** Every part of Neha is a Plugin — model, tools, Bot loop, Group — and any Plugin can replace any part or add to it. The Kernel that Plugins attach to is fixed.
- **The CLI does everything the GUI does.** Neha is agent-native: whatever a user can do in the GUI, a user or an agent can do from the CLI.
- **Anyone can use it.** The default experience stands on its own; plugins, configuration, and protocols stay out of sight until wanted.
- **Work surfaces.** Whatever a teammate produces appears in the Chat or Group where the work was given, as a property of the conversation itself.
- **Calm on the surface.** A Chat or a Group shows results, useful progress, and genuine requests; a Bot's internal tool activity stays collapsed until the user asks for it.
- **What builders make reaches everyone.** Plugins and templates flow from the people who build them to the people who use them.
- **Teammates work in the open.** They say everything to each other in the Group they share with people.
- **A Group is open to any agent that speaks its protocol.** Neha's own Bots are the first to speak it.
- **Authority comes from the machine's owner.** A teammate's authority on a machine is the owner's alone to grant: explicit, visible, revocable.
- **Approval is the acting User's.** When a Bot asks before acting, it asks where the work happens, and the User it acts for answers.
